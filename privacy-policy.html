Emalahleni App – Privacy Policy

Emalahleni Local Municipality · Effective 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Emalahleni App (Android and iPhone) collects, why, who can see it, and how you can see, correct or delete it. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who we are

Emalahleni Local Municipality (Civic Centre, President Street, eMalahleni, 1034, Mpumalanga, South Africa) is the responsible party for the personal information you give in the app. The Municipal Manager is the municipality's Information Officer.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and phone number, and, if you choose to give them, your ward and municipal account number.
  • Reports you send: the problem you describe, its location (from your phone's GPS or an address you type), photos you add, and your account number if you give it.
  • Tip-offs: the information and photos you choose to send. You can send a tip-off without giving your name.
  • Notifications: a notification token from Google Firebase (or, on Huawei phones, Huawei Push Kit) so that we can send report updates and municipal notices to your phone.

The app does not show advertisements, does not use an advertising ID, and does not sell your information.

Why we use it

To route your report to the right department, fix the problem, keep you updated, and send municipal notices. POPIA allows this processing so that the municipality can perform its public functions.

Who sees it

  • Municipal officials and service providers working on your report, who must keep it confidential.
  • Companies that run the app's systems for us: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Google Firebase or Huawei Push Kit (push notifications). They process the information only on our instructions.

Buttons for paying your account, buying prepaid electricity or contacting us on WhatsApp open other services (for example TsogaPay, EasyPay and WhatsApp), which have their own privacy policies.

How we protect it

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and only authorised municipal staff can open reports.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to resolve your report and to meet the laws on municipal record-keeping.

Delete your account

In the app: tap Profile at the bottom of the screen, then Delete my account, and confirm.

Without the app: email app@emalahleni.gov.za from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted: your login, your name, phone number, email address, ward and account number, your notifications and your phone's notification token.
What is kept: reports you already sent stay with the municipality so they can still be attended to and recorded as the law requires, but they are no longer linked to you.

Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Contact app@emalahleni.gov.za. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Children

The app is meant for residents aged 18 and older.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses personal information, and change the date at the top.
